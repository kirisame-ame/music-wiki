# Diminished Approach from Below

A diminished chord a semitone below a target chord can lead into it. Here, “step below” means one semitone: B° or B°7 resolves to C.

## Common forms

- **Leading-tone triad:** B°–C
- **Leading-tone seventh:** B°7–C or Cmaj7
- **Approach to another chord:** C♯°7–Dm7; E°7–F
- **Inversions:** Revoice the diminished seventh chord to keep its notes close to the target
