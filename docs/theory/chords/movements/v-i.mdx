# V–I: Dominant to Tonic

The dominant chord (V) resolves to the tonic (I). In C major, that is G or G7 moving to C. In a minor key, V commonly uses a raised leading tone: G7 to Cm.

## Common forms

- **V–I:** G–C
- **V7–I:** G7–C
- **V7sus4–V7–I:** G7sus4–G7–C
- **V7–i:** G7–Cm
