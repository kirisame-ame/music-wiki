# 2–5–1 Substitutions and Colors

Use these to change the voicing, bass motion, or harmonic color while keeping a path to the tonic.

## Degree 2

- **Extensions / voicings:** Dm9, Dm11, Dm13, Fmaj9/D
- **Minor color:** Dm7♭5
- **Borrowed or chromatic approaches:** B♭13, A♭maj7, B♭m(maj7)

## Degree 5

- **Extensions / inversions:** G13, G/B
- **Tritone substitute:** D♭7♯11 for G7
- **Diminished substitute:** Bdim7 for rootless G7♭9
- **Backdoor dominant color:** A♭/B♭ (B♭9sus4)

## Degree 1

- **Tonic colors:** Cmaj9, C13, Cmaj13♯11
- **Slash chords:** C/E, C/G, or B/Cmaj7
- **Modal borrowing:** Cm(maj7) or A♭maj7
- **Chromatic mediant color:** Amaj7
