# Minor 2–5–1

The common form is **iiø7–V7(♭9)–i**. In C minor: **Dø7–G7♭9–Cm**. The dominant uses B natural to lead into C.

## Common variations

- **iiø:** Dø7, Dø9, or Dø11
- **V:** G7♭9, G7♭13, or an altered G7
- **i:** Cm(maj7), Cm9, or Cm6/9
