# Tritone Substitution

A dominant seventh chord can be replaced by another dominant seventh chord whose root is a tritone away. In C, Db7 can replace G7 and resolve to C.

## Common forms

- **bII7–I:** Db7–Cmaj7
- **ii7–bII7–I:** Dm7–Db7–Cmaj7
- **Applied substitution:** A7–Eb7–Dm7
- **Extensions:** Db9 or Db7♯11 in place of Db7
